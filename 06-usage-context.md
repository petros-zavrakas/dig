# Usage context

> **Draft.** Compiled from the "Usage" section of every screen file. The frequencies,
> modes and devices are estimates from the domain docs and the seed data. **Petros to confirm
> or correct**, either here or in the screen files. The designer should use this to decide
> which screens get the most polish, the most density, and a mobile-first layout.

| Screen | Role | Frequency | Mode | Device |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [‹Rufname› — Trainings (a dog's training history)](04-screens/extranet/shared--dog-training-history.md) | Trainer:in and Trainingspartner:in: two mounts of the same page with different columns | Trainer:in a few times a week (preparing a session); Trainingspartner:in occasionally. | reading and scanning backwards in time, sometimes narrowing by training type or date range. Nothing is entered. | often the phone (cards); also desktop (table). |
| [Hunde / Meine Hunde](04-screens/extranet/shared--dogs.md) | Trainer:in („Hunde“) and Trainingspartner:in („Meine Hunde“): two mounts of the same list | Trainer:in a few times a week, typically before a training to look at what colleagues did with the dog; Trainingspartner:in occasionally. | finding one dog and tapping it. Nothing is entered. | mostly the phone, in the field; also desktop. |
| [Startseite](04-screens/extranet/shared--home.md) | Trainer:in, Trainingspartner:in, and an account holding both roles | every session starts here (login lands on it). Trainer:innen several times a week, Trainingspartner:innen roughly weekly. | a launcher. One tap, then away. Nothing is read here except the greeting. | mainly the phone, right before or after a training. |
| [Kein Zugriff](04-screens/extranet/shared--no-access.md) | Any signed-in account (see "Two variants") | rare. Mostly office staff who try the Extranet with their Intranet account, or an old bookmark. | read one sentence, press one button. | either. |
| [Training gespeichert](04-screens/extranet/shared--training-saved.md) | Trainer:in and Trainingspartner:in (one page component, mounted for each role) | after every capture. | a glance and one tap. | mainly the phone. |
| [Abgeschlossene Rechnungen — Abgeschlossene Eigenrechnungen](04-screens/extranet/trainer--billed-trainer-billing-runs.md) | Trainer:in | rare; a few times a year, to find an old invoice or download its PDF again. | looking up one invoice by number, date or period. | desktop and phone. |
| [Training (read from the dog's history)](04-screens/extranet/trainer--dog-file-training.md) | Trainer:in | occasional; a few times a week at most, while preparing a session with a dog. | reading one record, then back. | mostly the phone. |
| [Rechnung — Offene Trainings für die Eigenrechnung](04-screens/extranet/trainer--open-trainings.md) | Trainer:in | once a month or once a quarter, when she writes her invoice. | checking and selecting. She checks that each session has its travel declared, narrows to a period (often a quarter), ticks sessions and creates the run. No data entry on this page; a missing travel declaration is fixed through the session's date link. | desktop and phone both; on the phone this page is very long. |
| [Eigenrechnungs-Lauf](04-screens/extranet/trainer--trainer-billing-run.md) | Trainer:in | once per invoice (monthly or quarterly), one or two visits per run; closed runs are reopened rarely, mainly to download the PDF again. | checking the lines and total, typing two or three short values (hourly rate the first time, invoice number, invoice date), confirming. | desktop more likely (she writes the invoice in her own software), but the phone must work. |
| [Neues Training (Trainer:in)](04-screens/extranet/trainer--training-capture.md) | Trainer:in | the most-used screen of the Extranet: several times a week, right after each training. | data entry, quick. Typical entry: date (today, prefilled), duration, kind, one team, content ticks, travel-cost choice, save. Times, place and remark are optional. | mainly the phone, outdoors, often one-handed. |
| [Training — detail and edit (Trainer:in)](04-screens/extranet/trainer--training-detail.md) | Trainer:in (her own trainings) | a few times a week — mostly to add a forgotten detail, check the Trainingspartner:in's remark, or mark documentation done before invoicing. | mostly reading the facts at the top and changing one or two fields. | phone and desktop. |
| [Meine Trainings (Trainer:in)](04-screens/extranet/trainer--trainings.md) | Trainer:in | several times a week, usually right after recording a training or when preparing her own invoice. | scanning and finding one training; occasional filtering by dog or date range. | phone in the field and desktop at home, roughly equally (draft). |
| [Neues Training (Trainingspartner:in)](04-screens/extranet/training-partner--training-capture.md) | Trainingspartner:in | occasional — a few times a month at most; most trainings are recorded by the Trainer:in. | data entry, short: date, duration, team, Trainer:in, perhaps a remark. | mainly the phone. |
| [Training — detail and edit (Trainingspartner:in)](04-screens/extranet/training-partner--training-detail.md) | Trainingspartner:in (trainings of the teams she keeps) | occasional — after a training, to read the Trainer:in's remark or add her own. | mainly reading; typing one remark. | mainly the phone. |
| [Meine Trainings (Trainingspartner:in)](04-screens/extranet/training-partner--trainings.md) | Trainingspartner:in | weekly or less. | reading. Occasional filtering by dog when she has more than one. | mainly the phone (draft). |
| [Konto anlegen](04-screens/intranet/admin--account-create.md) | Administrator:in | rare — when someone joins. Sometimes several in a row (hence „Weiteres Konto anlegen“). | data entry: two text fields and a few checkboxes. | desktop. |
| [Konto (account detail)](04-screens/intranet/admin--account-detail.md) | Administrator:in | occasional, a few times a month. | reading the state, then one action. Editing is short (name, roles). | desktop. |
| [Konten](04-screens/intranet/admin--accounts.md) | Administrator:in | occasional — when someone joins, leaves, or forgets a password. A few times a month. | finding one account (by name), then opening it. Little scanning. | desktop. |
| [App shell — header, navigation drawer, user menu](04-screens/intranet/shared--app-shell.md) | Every signed-in role | on every page view. The drawer is opened at every change of area. | navigation only. | Intranet mainly desktop; Extranet phone and desktop. |
| [Übersicht (dashboard)](04-screens/intranet/shared--dashboard.md) | Every Intranet role (one page component for all; differences below) | seen at every sign-in; otherwise passed through quickly. | reading one line; one click for the Trainingskoordinator:in's quick action. | desktop. |
| [Hund anlegen — create a dog](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--dog-create.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator) | rare — a few times a year, when a litter or a self-training dog joins. Several dogs of one litter may be entered in one sitting. | data entry from paper or e-mail, 3–13 fields. | desktop. |
| [Hund — the dog file (all three Intranet roles)](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--dog-detail.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator), Kassenwart:in (Treasurer), Vorstand (Board) — one page per role, built from the same cards, see "Differences per role" | Trainingskoordinator:in often (several times a week); Kassenwart:in when checking one team's trainings before billing; Vorstand occasionally. | mostly reading. Writes are rare and small: a keeper change a few times a year per dog; releasing trainings for billing in batches. | desktop. |
| [Hunde — the dog list (all three Intranet roles)](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--dogs.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator), Kassenwart:in (Treasurer), Vorstand (Board) — one shared list component, see "Differences per role" | Trainingskoordinator:in several times a week (as the way into a dog file); Kassenwart:in and Vorstand occasionally. | looking up one dog by call name, or narrowing by litter, phase or entry year to get an overview. No data entry. | desktop. |
| [Person anlegen — create a person](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--person-create.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator) | a few times a month. | data entry, 2–9 fields, often from an application form or e-mail. | desktop. |
| [Person — the person file](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--person-detail.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator) | a few times a month, typically right after an administrator has created an account that now needs linking to its person. | reading, then one action (link or unlink). | desktop. |
| [Personen — the person directory](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--persons.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator) — no other role reaches this page | weekly or less; mostly as the way into a person's file to link or unlink an account, or to check whether a person already exists before creating one. | looking up one person by name. No data entry on this page. | desktop. |
| [Trainer:in anlegen — create a trainer](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--trainer-create.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator) | rare — a few times a year. | a choice, then data entry (2–11 fields for a new person, 0–2 for an existing one). | desktop. |
| [Trainer:in — the trainer page](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--trainer-detail.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator) | rare — seen once per created trainer. | a glance to confirm what was saved. | desktop. |
| [Training — detail and correction (Trainingskoordinator:in)](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--training-detail.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator) | several times a week, usually one record at a time after spotting something in the list. | reading first, then a targeted edit of one or two fields; occasionally a delete. | desktop. |
| [Trainings — the training list (all three Intranet roles)](04-screens/intranet/training-coordinator--trainings.md) | Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator), Kassenwart:in (Treasurer), Vorstand (Board) — one shared list component, see "Differences per role" | Trainingskoordinator:in and Kassenwart:in several times a week; the Vorstand occasionally. | mostly scanning and filtering (by Trainer:in, Hund, period, Abrechnungsstatus). In the edit mode the writing roles work row by row through a page: set the Kostenpflicht-Variante, press „Freigeben“, or release the whole page. | desktop. The table has up to 14 columns. |
| [Rechnungen — Offene abrechenbare Trainings](04-screens/intranet/treasurer--billable-trainings.md) | Kassenwart:in (Treasurer) | a few sessions per quarter, each long. Work happens in bursts, team by team. | scanning and selecting. She reads amounts, checks for warnings, ticks rows and creates a run. No data entry on this page. | desktop. The table is wide (up to 12 columns). |
| [Abgeschlossene Rechnungen](04-screens/intranet/treasurer--billed-club-billing-runs.md) | Kassenwart:in (Treasurer) | occasional — when a team asks about an invoice, at year end, or to download an attachment for sending or archiving. | looking up. Filter or search, then open a run or download a PDF. No data entry. | desktop. |
| [Abrechnungs-Lauf — one club billing run (in preparation and closed)](04-screens/intranet/treasurer--club-billing-run.md) | Kassenwart:in (Treasurer) | in preparation: once per team per billing period, usually straight after creating the run on „Rechnungen“. Closed: occasionally, to look up an invoice or download its attachment again. | in preparation: checking amounts, small edits, then one careful close (the number is typed from another program, the total compared against it). Closed: reading and downloading. | desktop. She works side by side with the association software. |
| [Tarife (Kassenwart:in and Trainingskoordinator:in)](04-screens/intranet/treasurer--tariffs.md) | Kassenwart:in (Treasurer) and Trainingskoordinator:in (Training coordinator) — identical page, see "Differences per role" | rare — once at setup, then about once a year when fees change. | reading the current values; occasionally editing a few amounts and saving. | desktop. |
| [Trainer-Abrechnung — Offene Trainings je Trainer:in](04-screens/intranet/treasurer--trainer-open-trainings.md) | Kassenwart:in (Treasurer) | whenever a trainer invoice arrives — a few per month, more at quarter end. | checking. Pick a Trainer:in (and „abgerechnet“ + the invoice number), compare the group sums with the invoice. No data entry. | desktop. |
| [Training — detail (Kassenwart:in and Vorstand)](04-screens/intranet/treasurer--training-detail.md) | Kassenwart:in (Treasurer); the Vorstand (Board) has a read-only version, see "Differences per role" | Kassenwart:in a few times a week, mostly when a training on a billing page needs a closer look; Vorstand rarely. | reading; for the Kassenwart:in one or two quick billing actions. | desktop. |
| [Anmeldung](04-screens/shared/shared--login.md) | Signed out | once per session; rare for people who stay signed in. | data entry, two fields. | Intranet desktop; Extranet mostly phone (Trainer:innen and Trainingspartner:innen in the field), also desktop. |
| [Neues Passwort vergeben (password reset)](04-screens/shared/shared--reset-password.md) | Signed out — the account holder following a password-reset e-mail | rare — once per forgotten password. | data entry, two fields, then one link. | whatever the e-mail is opened on; often a phone. |
| [Passwort vergeben (first password from an invitation)](04-screens/shared/shared--set-password.md) | Signed out — a new account holder following an invitation e-mail | once per account, ever. | data entry, two fields, then one link. This is often the person's **first contact** with the application. | whatever the e-mail is opened on; for Extranet accounts usually a phone. |
